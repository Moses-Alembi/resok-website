Membership

Join RESOK and help build a digitally enabled, financially resilient society advancing research, education, advocacy, partnerships, and policy advisory.

Why It Matters

Becoming a member of RESOK connects respiratory, pulmonary, and critical care professionals to the strategic plan’s five pillars: organizational sustainability, research, education and promotion, advocacy, partnerships and collaboration, and policy advisory.

Member Benefits

Gain access to exclusive resources, global networking, and cutting-edge clinical tools designed to elevate your career and patient care.

🏛️

International Conference

Members receive exclusive, heavily discounted admission to the annual RESOK International Conference, the premier gathering for pulmonary and critical care professionals.

📚

Leading Scientific Journals

Get full digital access to RESOK's peer-reviewed scientific journals, featuring the most-cited respiratory research and clinical guidelines in the field.

🔬

Research Grants & Funding

Apply for exclusive funding opportunities supporting basic, translational, and clinical research in pulmonary, critical care, and sleep medicine.

🌍

Partnerships & Collaboration

Connect with strategic partners and peers through structured alliances that support shared value, sustainability, and governance.

📢

Advocacy & Policy Advisory

Stay informed on advocacy priorities, policy briefs, and society positions that shape lung health action in Kenya and beyond.

🫺

Research, Education & Promotion

Access evidence-based guidelines, CPD learning, publications, and training pathways that support the national eLearning agenda.

Ready to Advance Respiratory Health?

Join thousands of dedicated professionals shaping the future of pulmonary and critical care medicine.
