Elections

ReSoK board elections. You can vote in an election if you were on the electoral roll when it was drawn — that is decided by your membership, not by when you sign in.

Nominate a candidate

You may nominate one member for each post, and you may nominate yourself. Nominating someone else asks them to accept before their name goes on the ballot.

Give their full details. They have no ReSoK account, so the returning officer will contact them on these to confirm they are willing to stand.

Your vote is final. Once you submit this ballot it cannot be changed or withdrawn, and nobody can change it for you. Check your choices on the next screen before confirming.
