Call for Abstracts

The 8th Kenya International Scientific Lung Health Conference (KISLHC 2027) invites abstracts for oral and poster presentation. Abstracts are submitted, reviewed and decided entirely online, through the ReSoK abstract system.

The conference brings together nurses, clinicians, researchers, public health practitioners, policymakers, implementers, innovators, and postgraduate students working across TB, lung health, and health systems.

Important Notice

The presenting author of every accepted abstract must register for the conference. An accepted abstract whose presenter has not registered by the set date may be offered to an abstract on the waiting list.

Acceptance of an abstract does not imply funding for travel or accommodation.

Abstracts sent by email are not accepted. Please use the submission system.

Eligibility

Abstract submissions are invited from:

  • All lung health and TB professionals at international, regional, national, county and facility levels
  • Researchers and academic institutions
  • Implementing partners and civil society organisations
  • Postgraduate students
  • Innovators and digital health practitioners
  • Policymakers and programme managers

You do not need to be a ReSoK member to submit. Members sign in with their portal login; everyone else creates a free account.

What to Submit

The submission form checks each of these as you type, so an abstract that breaks a limit cannot be submitted by mistake.

  • Title: up to 20 words
  • Abstract: up to 300 words in four sections: Background, Methods, Results, Conclusion. Italics, superscript and subscript are supported.
  • Keywords: 3 to 6
  • Figure: one table, figure or graph may be uploaded
  • Authors: list every author with their affiliation and mark one presenting author
  • Track: choose the one track that fits best
  • Presentation: oral, poster, or either. The Scientific Committee makes the final choice based on the content.
  • Declarations: the work is original, all co-authors agree to the submission, and conflicts of interest are disclosed
  • Language: English

Drafts are saved, and you can edit or withdraw a submitted abstract until the deadline.

How It Works

  1. Sign in, or create a free account, on the submission system.
  2. Write your abstract in the form. Save a draft at any time and come back to it.
  3. Submit before 31 May 2027, 23:59 East Africa Time. You receive a reference number by email.
  4. Each abstract is scored by two reviewers in a double-blind review: reviewers do not see the authors' names, and authors do not see the reviewers'.
  5. Every author is told the decision by email, and can see the status of each abstract by signing in.
  6. Presenting authors of accepted abstracts register for the conference, and their abstracts are placed in the programme as oral presentations or posters.
