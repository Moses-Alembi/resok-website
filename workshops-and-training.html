Training pathways

How ReSoK Builds Capacity

The workshop program combines procedure practice, diagnostic skills, clinical updates, and public education so learning can move directly into patient care.

Procedural Skills

Bronchoscopy, pleural care, and structured preparation for safe respiratory procedures.

Diagnostics

Spirometry and thoracic ultrasound learning for stronger clinical assessment.

Clinical Updates

Symposia and focused teaching on TB, asthma, and changing respiratory practice.

Public Engagement

Awareness activities that support prevention, early diagnosis, and continuity of care.
