The Friends of Chakaya campaign brings together supporters who want to open the door for talented medical students to learn, connect, and lead.

Your contribution helps sponsor students attending the 8th Kenya International Scientific Lung Health Conference (KISLHC 2027). The venue and dates will be announced soon.

The conference gathers pulmonologists, researchers, public health leaders, and students from across Kenya and beyond. By backing a student’s attendance, you are investing in the next generation of respiratory medicine and research.

About Prof. Jeremiah Chakaya

Professor Jeremiah Chakaya Muhwa is a world-renowned Kenyan pulmonologist and global health leader specializing in tuberculosis control and respiratory medicine. His career has combined clinical practice, research, teaching, and advocacy, making him one of the most respected voices in lung health in Kenya and internationally.

He earned his MBChB from the University of Nairobi in 1985 and completed his M.Med in Internal Medicine at the same university in 1992. He later advanced his respiratory medicine training at the Royal Brompton Hospital, University of London, and at Kyorin University Hospital in Tokyo, Japan.

Prof. Chakaya has served as the National TB Program Manager at Kenya's Ministry of Health, worked as a Chief Research Officer and lung disease researcher at KEMRI, and continues to teach at Kenyatta University School of Medicine. He currently serves as Chief Executive Officer and Technical Director of the Respiratory Society of Kenya (ReSoK), as Professor of Global Respiratory Health at the Liverpool School of Tropical Medicine, and as Chair of the planning committee for this conference.

His international leadership includes serving as President of the International Union Against Tuberculosis and Lung Disease, Chair of the Global Fund's Technical Review Panel, Chair of the WHO Strategic and Technical Advisory Group for TB, and Vice Chair of the Stop TB Partnership Coordinating Board. Through every role, he has advanced science, policy, and community action in the fight against TB and respiratory disease.

How will my donation be used?
Funds are directed toward helping medical students attend the conference, including registration, learning materials, and related participation costs.
Can I sponsor more than one student?
Yes. Larger contributions can support multiple students or help cover a fuller conference experience for one or more recipients.
Can donors outside Kenya contribute?
Yes. International supporters are welcome. If M-PESA is not convenient, please contact the ReSOK team for a suitable arrangement.
