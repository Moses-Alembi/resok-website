Staff records

Titles, departments, reporting managers and staff numbers. Staff edit their own contact details; these fields are yours. Deactivating someone signs them out and removes their access at once.

Where they may sign in from. Hold Ctrl (or Cmd) to pick more than one. None chosen means anywhere in Kenya.

Load the HR employee list

Paste three tab-separated columns: serial or employee number, name, and job title. Check the account matched to each row before applying. Omit anyone whose record should stay unchanged.

A printed SN is a row number, not an employee number. Only turn off title-only mode when the first column contains real employee numbers and you also intend to update counties.

Permissions

Every member of staff can use the directory, announcements, the helpdesk and their own pages. Tick extra permissions here. Changes sign the person out so they take effect at once. Super administrators hold all of them.

Activity log
