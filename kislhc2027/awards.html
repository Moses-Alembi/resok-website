KISLHC Awards

NOMINATIONS FOR AWARDS

Celebrate excellence in lung health by nominating outstanding contributors for the KISLHC 2027 awards. Nominations open closer to the conference; the dates will be published here.

Nominate Now Learn More
A. The Nomination Process

How nominations should be submitted

Individuals may be nominated by one or more individuals or organizations using the format provided by the conference organizers.

1

Prepare the nomination package

Include a brief outline of the nominee's work for consideration, limited to one page.

2

Attach the supporting documents

Include a brief CV for the nominee and the relevant work, capped at three pages.

3

Send to the secretariat

Email the complete nomination to kislcsecretariat@resok.org and info@resok.org.

4

Committee review

The nomination profiles will be forwarded to an independent Awards Committee for selection. Any committee member with a conflict of interest should declare it and step aside from that award decision.

The Tom Arocho Award

For commitment and excellence in the coverage of lung health issues in the media.

  • For a Kenyan journalist who has contributed immensely to lung health reporting.
  • May be through electronic media, print stories, or other journalistic coverage.
  • Stories may cover TB in Kenya, MDR-TB, government response, or tobacco and lung health.

The James Ang'awa Award

For excellence in advancing public health gains in lung health in Kenya.

  • For an individual with strong public health impact.
  • Recognizes policy, programme implementation, access to care, treatment outcomes, and quality of care.
  • Also recognizes improved equity, reduced financial barriers, and progress on gender or other imbalances.

The Job Bwayo Award

For science in lung health in Kenya.

  • For an individual contributing to scientific knowledge in lung health.
  • Highlights research policy, research programmes, and evidence that shaped practice and policy.
  • Celebrates work with major impact on lung health research in Kenya.

The Joseph Aluoch Young Scientist and Best Abstract Awards

For promising young scientists and outstanding conference abstracts.

  • Open to individuals aged 35 years and below.
  • The abstract must have been submitted for the conference and accepted by the scientific committee.
  • The highest score from the Awards Committee's scoring criteria receives the award.

The Luceta Community Actor Award

Presented by Stop TB-Kenya for outstanding community contribution to lung health.

  • For a community person who has made a positive impact through an original initiative.
  • May reflect lived experience, community advocacy, or a locally rooted innovation.
  • Recognizes practical work that has improved lung health outcomes in the community.

Top Abstract Contributor Award

For the delegate who submitted the highest number of accepted abstracts.

  • Honours dedication to research and knowledge sharing.
  • Recognizes the strongest contribution to the evidence base for improved lung health.
  • Celebrates consistency, productivity, and commitment to scientific exchange.
Awards history and committee

The stories behind the awards

A brief history of the awards and the people guiding the selection process.

Tom Arocho, media and public health

This award recognizes the important role of the media in informing the public about health threats, research findings, and policy responses. It honours the legacy of Tom Arocho, remembered for reaching audiences with messages of hope and encouragement.

James Ang'awa and Job Bwayo

James Ang'awa is remembered for leadership in TB control and broader public health gains, while Job Bwayo is honored for lifelong dedication to research, evidence generation, and the pursuit of scientific solutions in lung health.

Luceta and the community legacy

Luceta's award reflects courage, resilience, advocacy, and the power of lived experience. It celebrates community voices that strengthen the fight against TB and inspire others to keep pushing for better care.
