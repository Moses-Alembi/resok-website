About ReSoK

Advancing lung health in Kenya and beyond.

The Respiratory Society of Kenya is a professional society advancing the 2026-2035 Strategic Plan through prevention, research, education, advocacy, multidisciplinary collaboration, and policy advisory.

Who We Are Core Values Membership
Who we are

Formerly KAPTLD, ReSoK is Kenya's professional home for respiratory health leadership.

The Respiratory Society of Kenya (ReSoK), formerly Kenya Association for the Prevention of Tuberculosis and Lung Disease (KAPTLD), is a professional organization with a membership constituted of health professionals and service providers with an interest in respiratory medicine, mainly chest medicine specialists.

Registered SocietyRegistered in Kenya as a society by the Registrar of Societies.
Professional MembershipConstituted by healthcare professionals and service providers focused on lung health.
National Reference RoleFocused on the strategic pillars of organizational sustainability, research and education, advocacy, partnerships, and policy advisory.
Leadership

Governance and Secretariat

ReSoK combines elected professional leadership with a secretariat that supports daily operations, programs, and technical coordination.

Registered in Kenya as a Society by the Registrar of Societies.

Leadership is provided by an elected board of management comprising mainly chest specialists, with representation from other cadres of the healthcare profession including Clinical Officers, Nurses, and Pharmacists.

The secretariat is led by a Chief Executive Officer who also acts as Technical Advisor, supported by a Programs Manager and an Administration & Finance Manager responsible for day-to-day operations.

  • Elected board of management providing strategic leadership.
  • Multidisciplinary representation across respiratory healthcare cadres.
  • Secretariat support for technical, program, administration, and finance functions.
Direction

Vision and Mission

The society's mandate is defined by the strategic plan: a clear vision, a practical mission, and PUMZI values that guide every decision.

Our Vision

Healthy lungs for all people in Kenya and beyond.

Our Mission

We innovatively promote, improve, sustain, and integrate quality lung health for all through prevention, research, education, advocacy, and multidisciplinary collaborations.

Our core values

What Guides ReSoK

These values are the PUMZI foundation of the strategic plan and shape how the society collaborates with members, partners, communities, and the wider health system.

Partnerships

Partnerships keep ReSoK connected to members, institutions, and communities working toward the same lung health goals.

Understanding

Understanding helps the society listen carefully and respond to the needs of professionals, patients, and partners.

Meticulousness

Meticulousness anchors ReSoK in careful planning, evidence, and precise execution across every program.

Zeal

Zeal keeps the society energetic, committed, and persistent in advancing respiratory health.

Innovation

Innovation pushes ReSoK to build digitally enabled, sustainable, and forward-looking solutions.
