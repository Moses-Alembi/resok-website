SPONSORSHIP OPPORTUNITIES

Why Partner With Us?

The 8th Kenya International Scientific Lung Health Conference (KISLHC 2027) is a premier platform for stakeholders in lung health and respiratory medicine.

By partnering with us, you will:

  • Gain visibility among 500+ healthcare professionals, researchers, and policymakers from across Africa and beyond.
  • Position your brand as a leader in lung health innovation.
  • Access a highly engaged audience through our conference materials, digital channels, and on-site branding.
  • Contribute to advancing lung health in Kenya and globally.

We offer tailored sponsorship packages to meet your goals — from logo placement to exclusive content opportunities.
