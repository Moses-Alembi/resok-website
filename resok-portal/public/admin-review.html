Attendance & CPD tokens

1. Record who attended

Paste a Zoom participant export, a typed register, or a column from a spreadsheet. Any line containing an email address is read; headers and summary rows are ignored. Pasting twice is safe — people are matched on their email, never duplicated.

2. Load the KMPDC tokens

Paste the tokens generated on the KMPDC portal, one per line. They are encrypted before storage. Loading the same batch twice adds nothing — duplicates are dropped, so no two people can be handed the same token.

3. Hand them out

Gives one token to each person marked as attended who does not already have one. Nobody can collect a token until this is done.
