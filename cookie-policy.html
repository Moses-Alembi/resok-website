What cookies are

A cookie is a small file a website stores in your browser. Some are needed for a site to work at all — keeping you signed in, for instance. Others are optional, and exist to tell the site’s owners how it is being used.

We treat that distinction seriously: optional cookies are not set unless you accept them, and the site works completely without them.

Strictly necessary cookies

These are required for the site and the members’ portal to function. They cannot be switched off, because without them you could not sign in or stay signed in.

NameLifetimePurpose
resok_token20 minutes of inactivityKeeps you signed in to the members’ portal. It is signed, is marked HttpOnly so scripts cannot read it, and is sent only over encrypted connections.
resok_cookie_consentUntil you clear itStored in your browser rather than sent to us. It remembers the choice you make below, so you are not asked on every page.

Analytics cookies — optional

With your permission we use Google Analytics to understand which pages are useful and where people struggle. This helps us decide what to improve. These are set only after you accept them.

NameLifetimePurpose
_gaUp to 2 yearsDistinguishes one visitor from another
_gid24 hoursDistinguishes one visitor from another
_gat1 minuteLimits how often requests are sent

We ask Google to anonymise your IP address, because the Act treats an IP address as personal data and the Society has no use for it at full precision.

Changing your mind

You can change your choice whenever you like — there is no penalty for refusing, and nothing on this site is withheld from you for it.

If you refuse, or later withdraw consent, any analytics cookies already set are deleted from your browser.

Through your browser

Every major browser also lets you block or delete cookies in its settings. Doing so for strictly necessary cookies will prevent you from signing in to the portal.

Questions

Our Privacy Policy explains more broadly what we collect and the rights you hold. For anything else, write to info@resok.org.
