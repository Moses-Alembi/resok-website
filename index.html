Respiratory Society of Kenya

The Respiratory Society of Kenya (ReSoK) formerly Kenya Association for the Prevention of Tuberculosis and Lung disease (KAPTLD) is a professional organization with a membership constituted of health professionals and service providers with an interest in respiratory medicines mainly chest medicine specialists...

Advancing Lung Health Through Care, Research & Advocacy

The Respiratory Society of Kenya brings together health professionals and partners to improve quality lung health through prevention, education, research, advocacy, and multidisciplinary collaboration.

20+ Years of Excellence

Our Mission

To promote, improve, sustain, and integrate quality lung health for all through prevention, research, education, advocacy, and multidisciplinary collaborations.

Our Vision

Healthy lungs for all people in Kenya and beyond.
