7th KISLHC 2026

The next Kenya International Scientific Lung Health Conference will take place from 5th to 8th August 2026 at Diani Reef Beach Hotel, Kwale County, Kenya.

From Detection to Recovery: Integrated Pathways & Patient-Centered Innovations in Lung Health

KISLHC 2026 convenes clinicians, researchers, public health leaders, civil society, academia, private sector partners, and communities to accelerate progress toward ending TB by 2030 and improving lung health across Kenya and beyond.

Dates5-8 Aug 2026
VenueDiani Reef Beach Hotel
LocationKwale, Kenya
  • Review achievements and identify gaps in chronic lung disease management.
  • Promote implementation and scale-up of new WHO-recommended tools in lung health.
  • Mobilize political and financial commitment for lung health research, innovation, and care.
  • Address health inequities, human rights, stigma, and public health pathways toward universal health coverage.

The programme includes pre-conference workshops, plenaries, symposia, tracks, and e-poster sessions across four days.
