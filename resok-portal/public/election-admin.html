New election

The nomination window is optional. Leave both dates empty to enter a slate agreed elsewhere; fill them in to let members put names forward. Nominations must close before voting opens.

Electoral roll

Drawn once and frozen. Only members who paid for the year chosen and can sign in reach it — an entry for somebody who cannot open the portal is not an entitlement to vote, it is a turnout figure that can never be reached.

Posts and candidates

Being first on a ballot paper is worth votes, so the order is randomised rather than alphabetical — and frozen with everything else when voting opens.

Nominations

Approving a nomination puts that name on the ballot. A nomination the nominee has not accepted is shown as waiting — standing for a board should not happen to somebody without their agreement.

The count

Not available until voting has closed, and every reading of it is logged. A tie for the last seat stops a declaration rather than being resolved by a sort order.
