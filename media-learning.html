Learning media

Videos, podcasts, and case-based learning.

A ReSoK media channel can turn social updates, webinars, workshops, guidelines, and expert interviews into organised learning resources.

Channel formats

Instead of relying only on social feeds, ReSoK can publish durable educational content that members, patients, and partners can find later.

Recorded CMEs

Webinar recordings, conference highlights, practical demonstrations, and workshop summaries grouped by topic.

Guidelines in Practice

Short conversations where experts explain how guideline recommendations apply in real clinical settings.

Case of the Month

Structured clinical cases for members, with history, investigation, decision points, and learning notes.

Editorial calendar

A simple monthly rhythm can keep the site alive: one patient guide, one clinician brief, one expert video, and one member case discussion.

Patient guide

Plain-language health education linked to current awareness months.

Clinician brief

A practical diagnostic or treatment pathway for front-line teams.

Member learning

A CPD-linked talk, case, or workshop recap for professional development.
