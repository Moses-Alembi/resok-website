Patient and public education

Plain-language lung health resources.

Patient-friendly information that helps people recognise symptoms, seek care early, understand tests, and manage respiratory conditions with confidence.

Patient education library

These topics can become downloadable one-page guides for hospitals, clinics, schools, community health workers, and public campaigns.

When to seek care

Warning signs that need urgent attention.

  • Severe breathlessness
  • Chest pain
  • Coughing blood
  • Persistent fever or weight loss

Asthma and inhalers

Simple guides for inhaler technique, trigger control, action plans, and school support.

Smoking cessation

Clear advice on quitting tobacco, second-hand smoke, vaping risks, and where to find help.

TB and cough awareness

Reducing stigma and encouraging early testing for persistent cough and TB symptoms.

Lung cancer warning signs

Information on persistent cough, unexplained weight loss, chest symptoms, and referral.

Clean air at home

Indoor smoke, cooking fuels, ventilation, occupational exposure, and pollution prevention.

Medical safety note: patient resources should support, not replace, consultation with a qualified health professional. Emergency symptoms require urgent care.
