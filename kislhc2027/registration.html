Registration

Registration Opens Soon

Registration for KISLHC 2027 is not open yet. Fees, categories, early-bird dates and the pre-conference workshops will be announced on this page.

How Registration Will Work

  1. One account. Register with your ReSoK account, the same login used for abstract submission. Members of the Society sign in with their portal login; everyone else creates a free account.
  2. Choose your category and any pre-conference workshops. ReSoK members in good standing receive the member rate.
  3. Pay online by M-Pesa, or by bank transfer for institutions and groups.
  4. Receive your QR code. Once payment is confirmed, your confirmation email carries a personal QR code.
  5. Collect your badge at the venue. The QR code is printed on it and scanned at each session, which also records your attendance for CPD points.

Presenting an Abstract?

The call for abstracts opens on 1 November 2026 and closes on 31 May 2027. The presenting author of every accepted abstract must register for the conference.
