Attendance and CPD

Each day, everyone scanned in becomes an in-person attendee of the CPD event you choose for that day. Load that event's KMPDC tokens on the Events page as usual and they go to these people. You can send again after late scans; nobody is added twice.

Registration categories

Each category has one or more fees by date, for example Early bird until 30 June, then Standard, then Onsite. The fee that applies on the day a delegate registers is what they pay. Leave a start or end date empty for "from the beginning" or "until the end".

Workshops and extras

Group invoices

For an institution paying for several delegates. Create the group and send its join code to the institution: delegates enter it when they register, and are not asked to pay. You can also add an existing registration by its reference. Download the invoice for the institution; when it pays, record the payment once and every member is confirmed with their own QR code.

Sponsors and exhibitors

Logos appear on the conference site by tier. Booth staff sign in to the booth lead scanner to save delegates who visit their stand; they see a delegate's email only if the delegate agreed at registration. Booth staff need their own conference registration (use a staff-only category) to get a badge.

Delegate evaluation

Open the evaluation when the conference ends. Only delegates who were scanned in can answer, once each, from their conference page. Each is emailed an invitation within the hour, and one reminder five days later if they have not answered. Answers are shown here without names.
