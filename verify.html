Verify a certificate

Enter the code printed on a ReSoK Virtual Academy certificate to check that it is genuine.

The code is at the bottom left of the certificate.

A valid result means the Respiratory Society of Kenya issued this certificate to the person named, for the course shown. It confirms that they completed that course. It is not a licence or a professional qualification.
